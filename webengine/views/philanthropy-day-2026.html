We want to celebrate the work behind the mission.

Some of the most important nonprofit work never makes the headlines.

It happens when a volunteer stays late.

When a family gets the support it needed.

When a program reaches one more community.

When a team finds a way forward despite limited resources, growing needs, and difficult circumstances.

National Philanthropy Day is an opportunity to recognize the organizations behind those moments.

Tell us what your nonprofit made possible this year.

We’ll help selected organizations bring that work to a bigger audience.

What should you share?

You don’t need a perfectly polished story or a major national campaign.

We want to hear about real work and real impact.

You might tell us about:

A community you reached

Did your organization expand into a new neighborhood, serve more people, or reach a group that previously lacked access?

A program that made a difference

Did you launch something new, grow an existing program, or see a meaningful outcome from your work?

A challenge your organization took on

What problem is becoming more urgent — and what is your team doing about it?

A milestone worth celebrating

Maybe you mobilized volunteers, expanded services, reached a fundraising goal, helped more families, or achieved something your team has worked toward for years.

A moment that reminded you why the work matters

Sometimes one person, one family, one volunteer, or one community moment says more about your mission than a dozen statistics.

If it mattered to your community, we want to hear about it.
