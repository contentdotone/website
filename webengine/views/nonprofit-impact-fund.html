Why we created the fund

You focus on making an impact. We want to help make more of it possible.

Every day, nonprofits take on challenges that matter deeply to their communities.

They bring people together, fund critical programs, mobilize supporters, share stories that deserve to be heard, and turn generosity into measurable change.

But meaningful work also takes resources.

It takes successful fundraising campaigns. Strong relationships. The right partners. Technology that supports growth rather than slowing it down. And organizations willing to step forward and contribute.

We created the Nonprofit Impact Fund to do more of that work — and to make our resources, expertise, and network available to more organizations advancing important missions.

Because when nonprofits succeed, entire communities benefit.

A little more about the Nonprofit Impact Fund.

What is the Content.One Nonprofit Impact Fund?

It’s Content.One’s initiative to support nonprofit organizations and the communities they serve through selected sponsorships, in-kind technology support, strategic guidance, educational resources, and collaborative opportunities.

Our aim is to contribute to the fundraising success, growth, and long-term sustainability of mission-driven organizations.

What kinds of organizations does the Fund support?

We welcome conversations with registered nonprofit organizations, foundations, nonprofit networks, fundraising teams, and mission-driven organizations.

We’re particularly interested in opportunities where our resources, expertise, or relationships can contribute meaningfully to an organization’s goals.

Can our nonprofit request sponsorship for a gala, event, or program?

Absolutely. We welcome conversations about nonprofit events, fundraising initiatives, community programs, and educational opportunities.

Sponsorships and contributions are considered individually, based on the nature of the opportunity and available resources.

Does the Fund provide direct financial grants?

The Fund is primarily focused on in-kind technology support, applicable credits or discounts, strategic resources, and selected sponsorship opportunities rather than unrestricted cash grants. The specific form of support depends on the opportunity.

Do we need to be a Content.One customer?

You don’t need to be an existing Content.One customer to start a conversation with us.

Some forms of technology support may be tied to particular services or eligibility requirements, but we’re happy to explore your organization’s needs and determine whether there’s a relevant opportunity to work together.

Can Content.One help us review our existing technology and vendor spending?

Yes. Our team can discuss your existing digital ecosystem and explore opportunities for improving efficiency, reducing unnecessary costs, or identifying appropriate technology partners. The scope and availability of a deeper review are determined individually.

How do we get started?

Simply reach out through our Contact Us page to start a conversation, or email hello@content.one with a little information about your organization and what you’re hoping to accomplish.

No separate application is required.
