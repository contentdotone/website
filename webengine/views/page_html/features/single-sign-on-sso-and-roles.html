Start with roles everyone understands

Content.One ships with a spectrum of preconfigured roles so you can launch quickly with the right guardrails. Each role maps to common responsibilities across your organization.

Owner

Full access across Content, Schema, Media, Code, Leads, Redirects, Reports, Apps, Settings, and Accounts. Launch instances, add domains, invite users, and manage teams.

Admin

Same access as Owner without the ability to delete other users. Perfect for operational leads who manage day-to-day governance.

Developer

Code-first teams with access to build models, components, redirects, and integrations. Content, Schema, Media, Code, Leads, Redirects, Reports, Apps, Settings.

Developer Contributor

All Developer access without publish permissions. Ideal for contractors or QA engineers contributing safely.

SEO

Focused on discoverability with access to Content, Media, Leads, Redirects, Reports, and Apps. Empower growth teams to optimize without touching code.

Publisher

Content, Media, Leads, Reports, and Apps access with publish rights. Perfect for regional marketing teams with go-live responsibility.

Contributor

Create and edit in Content, Media, and Apps without publishing rights. Submit workflow requests to escalate approvals to publishers.

Access Admin

Specialized administrative view for managing identities and tokens. Grant to security teams who oversee account-wide governance.
