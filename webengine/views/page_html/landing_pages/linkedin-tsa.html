The Federation Blueprint

Concise points to consider while unifying your nonprofit locations — built for national teams who need consistency without losing local identity.

Define the federation rules

Clarify which decisions are national, regional, and local — and codify them in your CMS.

Unify data, not just pages

Standardize locations, services, and contacts so experiences stay accurate everywhere.

Blueprint Checklist

  • 1 Establish a shared design system and reusable components.
  • 2 Create cascades for campaigns, policies, and core content.
  • 3 Protect local flexibility with approved content zones.
  • 4 Instrument reporting so national teams see adoption in real time.
  • 5 Align roles, training, and support to match the new structure.
