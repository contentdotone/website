ADA compliance

We take care of ADA compliance.

Website accessibility is mandatory for many healthcare organizations. Failing to meet applicable ADA requirements can create legal and financial risk. Content.One builds accessibility into the website experience — reducing the work placed on your internal team.

  • Pages are structured for screen readers.
  • Navigation can be used without a mouse.
  • Text and buttons are designed for readable contrast.
  • Image uploads prompt the editor to add alternative text.
