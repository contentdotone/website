ADA compliance

Accessibility baked into every page.

Every page template Content.One ships with is WCAG 2.2 AA-aligned by default — the accessibility standard the ADA references for digital experiences. Rural hospitals get an ADA-compliant website out of the box, without a specialist review after every content update.

  • Semantic HTML, heading hierarchy, and landmark regions on every template.
  • Color contrast, text sizing, and focus states meeting WCAG 2.2 AA thresholds.
  • Keyboard-navigable everything: nav, forms, service pages, provider profiles.
  • Alt-text prompts on every image upload — admins get a nudge, not a manual.
  • Ongoing accessibility monitoring so gaps get flagged as your content grows.

Real talk: no vendor makes your site legally bulletproof — but Content.One removes the "we haven't looked at this in three years" gap that most rural hospital sites are living inside.

HIPAA compliance

Designed around your PHI boundaries.

Content.One is a HIPAA-designed marketing website layer. PHI stays where it belongs — in your EHR, your patient portal, or your approved system — and the website is engineered to link out cleanly instead of ever touching sensitive data.

  • Portal, bill pay, and forms link out to your HIPAA-authorized systems (TruBridge, MyCareCorner, ClearBalance, FastHealth).
  • No PHI collection on the marketing site — appointment intake goes straight to the authorized platform.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest across the platform.

  • Automatic security patching + 99.99% uptime handled by Content.One — no IT team required to maintain HIPAA posture.

Straightforward framing: Content.One is designed for HIPAA compliance at the marketing website layer. Actual PHI handling stays with the EHR/portal/approved system your hospital already runs.
