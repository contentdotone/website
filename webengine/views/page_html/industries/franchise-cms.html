Frequently asked questions

Questions that come up most often with franchise and chain operations evaluating Content.One.

How do you support both corporate control and local flexibility?

Templates, fields, and components are scoped by role. Corporate locks the parts of the page that have to stay consistent — disclaimers, brand voice, layout — and opens the rest to franchisees and field marketers. Locals never see what they're not supposed to touch.

Can a single campaign go out to every location at once?

Yes. Schedule a national campaign once at the system level and it propagates to every location site automatically. Locations can stage and preview before it goes live; corporate sees a single status across the rollout.

How does Content.One handle franchisee onboarding?

New franchisees get a pre-scoped role, a set of templates, and access to only the location records that belong to them. SSO and SCIM provisioning are included, so adding or removing a franchisee is a single change in your IdP.

Does it work with our existing front end?

Yes — Content.One is composable, headless, or hybrid. REST + GraphQL APIs and structured content models mean your existing Next.js, React, or proprietary front end can read from Content.One without a rewrite.

How is SEO managed across thousands of location pages?

Location, region, and service-area structured data is built into the content model — so every location page ships with consistent metadata, schema.org markup, and canonical patterns. Corporate sets the patterns once; every page follows.

What's included in enterprise pricing?

Dedicated support management, a 99.99% uptime SLA, named on-call engineers, migration engineering against your timeline, single tenancy, SOC 2 Type II, and bring-your-own-model AI. Volume packaging is sized to the number of locations and brands you operate.
