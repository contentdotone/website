Frequently asked questions

Questions that come up most often when banks, fintechs, credit unions, and wealth managers evaluate Content.One.

How is Content.One different from the CMS our bank runs today?

A traditional CMS ties every rate change and product launch to a development sprint. Content.One puts your landing pages, product content, and disclosures on one platform where product, growth, and compliance teams work with shared visibility. Marketing ships at market speed; compliance sees every change.

Do our marketing and product teams need developers to publish?

No. Teams spin up offer pages and microsites in minutes with brand-approved, drag-and-drop Blocks. Developers set up templates and endpoints once; after that, business teams update copy, rates, and campaigns without a new build.

What are Blocks, and why do they matter for a financial institution?

Blocks are reusable components — rate tables, product details, required disclosures — that compliance approves once and every team uses everywhere. Update a Block and it updates on every page, market, and brand that uses it. Growth teams move fast, and mandated language holds.

Does it support dual control and approval checkpoints?

Yes. Workflow approvals, dual control, and publishing checkpoints apply to every asset, so no regulated content ships on a single set of hands. Those controls are why your teams can publish quickly: the review is built into the path, not bolted on after.

What do we show examiners and internal audit?

A complete record: who changed what, when, and who approved it, on every content action. Audit logs export for internal and external reviews, and version history makes any change traceable and reversible. When an examiner asks, the answer is a report, not a reconstruction.

How granular is access control?

Permissions scope down to individual content items, API keys and tokens are managed per integration, and SSO keeps authentication with your identity team. Compliance edits disclosures; marketing edits campaigns; neither steps on the other.

How fast can we launch a new product page, offer, or campaign?

Days faster than a traditional release cycle. Pages assemble from approved Blocks, so a promo, onboarding flow, or lifecycle journey doesn’t wait on engineering. Publishing is controlled, logged, and reversible.

Rates changed this morning. How fast can every page reflect it?

Automatically, if you want. Webhooks and APIs sync rate and product availability directly from core systems, and shared rate-table Blocks cascade one approved update to every page that uses it. No page-by-page edits, no stale rates in market.

Can AI localize offers across regions and languages?

Yes. AI drafts translations of offers and disclosures for every market while mandated language stays preserved in each one. Regional and compliance teams review before publish.

Can the same content power our website, app, and branches?

Yes. One governed content source serves web, mobile, partner portals, advisor workstations, kiosks, and in-branch displays through REST, GraphQL, and custom endpoints. Update once; every channel reflects it.
