Content.One + CauseCircle
CauseCircle

One commitment. Two ways we put it to work.

Content.One and CauseCircle are committed to advancing diversity, equity, inclusion, and access—in how we work, what we build, and the communities we support. Together, we help nonprofits share stories more equitably, make digital participation more accessible, and ensure the people closest to a cause can help shape its narrative. We know this work is ongoing, and we remain committed to listening, learning, and taking meaningful action.

The work has to show up in the work.

Our approach is ongoing, practical, and open to improvement.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of initiatives does Content.One consider?

We consider nonprofit events, education programs, community gatherings, and DEI/IDEA initiatives aligned with access, storytelling, technology, fundraising, and mission-driven work.

What kinds of support are available?

Support may include sponsorship, a speaker, event participation, practical expertise, or another form of partnership. Availability depends on the initiative and timing.

Who can request a speaker?

Nonprofits, associations, conference organizers, community groups, and mission-aligned organizations are welcome to contact us.

Does submitting a request guarantee support?

No. Every request is reviewed for fit, timing, and available resources.
