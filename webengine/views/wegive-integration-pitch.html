Where most non-profits are stuck.

A donation page in one tool. A CRM in another. Marketing automation somewhere else. Local chapters running their own donor pages without telling HQ. Stewardship handled by spreadsheets and goodwill.

A patchwork of giving platforms, marketing tools, and CRM imports — every handoff a place where donor intent gets lost.

Manual data hygiene

Development and IT spend hours reconciling donor records each week. Marketing can't trust the segmentation.

Fragmented websites

Each chapter, region, or program has its own site, its own forms, its own brand drift. HQ has no central lever.

Stewardship after the fact

When the giving stack and the website stack don't talk, donors feel it — and so does recurring revenue.
