Conversation starter

Meet CauseCircle.

CauseCircle is a content collection app built by Content.One for nonprofits. It gives you a simple, mobile-first way to gather stories from the people around your mission — staff, volunteers, donors, program recipients, board members — and turn them into the kind of raw, real material that funders, grantors, and search engines actually respond to.

We'll have it open on a couple of iPads for anyone curious. Not to sell — just so you can see the pattern and steal the idea for your team. Story capture is one of the most underused levers in nonprofit content, and it doesn't need a big platform to start.

The bigger idea

Your website isn't a brochure anymore. It's fundraising infrastructure.

Ten years ago, a nonprofit website was a place to send people who already knew about you. Today, it's the first serious interaction most donors, foundations, and even journalists have with your organization — and increasingly, with AI systems that summarize your work before a human ever reads a word you wrote.

That means the site is doing more work than any brochure ever did: donor acquisition, retention, program visibility, trust-building, grant credibility, executive storytelling, chapter federation, board reporting. It's the fastest, cheapest, and most compounding piece of infrastructure most nonprofits own.

Content.One is where a lot of that infrastructure gets built — a content platform designed around structured storytelling, federated multi-site networks, and being readable by both humans and AI. Not something we need you to buy at happy hour. Just context for why we care about the topic, and why the conversations we're setting up matter to us.
