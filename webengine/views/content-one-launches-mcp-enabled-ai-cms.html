Content.One Launches MCP-Enabled AI CMS for Multi-Site Organizations

Content.One announced general availability of its MCP server, enabling marketers to launch websites through natural language prompts. The platform reduces enterprise workflows from 32 hours to approximately one hour.

SAN DIEGO, CA — June 15, 2026: Content.One today announced general availability of the Content.One MCP Server at mcp.content.one and its agentic interface at agent.content.one, turning the platform into an MCP-enabled AI CMS for the multi-site organizations it serves — franchises, multi-chapter nonprofits, multi-location brands, and federated enterprises.

A short demo of agent.content.one and the MCP server.

Key Capabilities

The system allows users to:

  • Generate content schemas in minutes by describing data model requirements — primary fields, relationships, headless data sets, and seeded placeholder content arrive as a review-and-approve proposal.
  • Create complete pages within one to two minutes, including articles, images, and design formatting applied from a stored brand context.
  • Build HTML components from design screenshots — paste an image, get the matching component ready to drop into any template.
  • Construct entire websites end-to-end in roughly 90 minutes, with full content, components, and structure assembled from scratch.
  • Install tracking pixels and scripts without developer assistance — paste a Meta Pixel or any script and choose where it installs (globally, on a model, or on a single page).
  • Optimize and organize images through simple uploads — Content.One names, optimizes, and indexes uploads in the media library automatically.
  • Run SEO diagnostics across multiple pages with the new SEO Analyzer app — surfaces missing Open Graph tags, duplicate H1s, and similar issues, applies fixes, and tracks scores over time.
  • Control the full application suite via natural language commands, including multilingual prompting (English, Spanish, Dutch, German). Covers Calendar, Workflows, Publisher, Forms, Pop-up manager, and the drag-and-drop builder.

Multi-Location Benefits

The MCP server exposes the platform's tools to AI-compatible clients while maintaining role-based access controls and permission structures. Each franchise, chapter, or regional team can operate independently while remaining under central oversight.

Security and Infrastructure

The platform maintains role-based access controls, SSO integration, full audit logging, and GDPR/CCPA compliance. A globally distributed CDN supports 99.999% availability for high-traffic properties.

Organizations can request a demonstration at www.content.one.

About Content.One

Content.One is an AI-powered enterprise content platform built for federated organizations managing complex, multi-site digital operations. The platform unifies multi-site website management, agentic page creation, schema design, document governance, digital asset management, and enterprise compliance controls into a single secure and scalable system — allowing organizations to reduce production costs, lower technology headcount, and shorten time-to-market across digital channels. Content.One serves clients across insurance, government, financial services, nonprofit, and franchise sectors. For more information, visit www.content.one.
