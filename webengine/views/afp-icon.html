🌊 San Diego Gems

San Diego Beyond
the Convention Center

You're in one of America's best cities. These are the spots worth making time for — a few minutes away, or a short drive, if you build them around conference gaps.

5
City picks
Walkable
Any time5 min walk
The Gaslamp Quarter

San Diego's historic Victorian commercial district, right at the SDCC doorstep. The best blocks for dinner are 4th and 5th Avenue between Broadway and Harbor. For a quieter post-session meal away from conference crowds, head one block east of 5th to Market Street — better restaurants, fewer lanyards.

Adjacent to SDCC5-min walk
Museums + Park
Morning/Evening~15 min drive
Balboa Park

One of the country's great urban parks. The Mingei International Museum (reopened after a major renovation) is exceptional — its collection of folk art and craftsmanship from around the world is a rare experience. The San Diego Museum of Art and the surrounding Spanish Colonial architecture make this worth a morning or evening trip.

Balboa Park, San Diego~15 min drive or Uber from SDCC
Harbor + Views
Any morningAdjacent
San Diego Bay Embarcadero

The waterfront walk along Harbor Drive is one of the most restorative 30 minutes you can have between conference sessions. The USS Midway Museum is right there if you have 90 minutes — it's genuinely remarkable. Early morning walk along the harbor before day sessions is underrated for clearing your head.

Harbor Drive · adjacent to SDCCWalkable
Food + Market
Any lunch/dinner10 min drive
Little Italy

San Diego's best neighborhood for a meal away from the conference energy. Herb & Wood is excellent for a dinner that feels like a genuine treat. The Little Italy Mercato (Saturdays, 8am–2pm on Date St) is one of California's best farmers' markets — worth an early Saturday morning if you can make it work.

Little Italy, San Diego~10 min drive or Uber
Coastal
Half-day20 min drive
La Jolla Cove & Torrey Pines

La Jolla Cove is a protected marine reserve — sea lions on the rocks, snorkeling, dramatic cliff walks. Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve offers short spectacular coastal hikes above the Pacific. For cultivation dinners with San Diego-based major gift prospects, The Lodge at Torrey Pines is a Craftsman masterpiece.

La Jolla, CA 92037~14 miles north of SDCC · 20–30 min drive
